Hsiao, Chun-En (蕭群恩)

Undergraduate Researcher · Embodied AI · Vision-Language-Action & World Action Models

I am an undergraduate student in the Arete Honors Program at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU), majoring in Artificial Intelligence in Engineering and Science.

I am currently an undergraduate researcher at the BASIC Lab, advised by Prof. Hong-Han Shuai (帥宏翰). My current research centers on vision-language-action (VLA) models and world action models (WAM) for embodied AI — studying how agents perceive, reason, and act in interactive environments.

My research is driven by a simple question: as AI systems become more capable and autonomous, how do we make them behave reliably under distribution shift and real-world uncertainty? I study this through embodied AI — how vision-language-action and world action models perceive, reason, and act — drawing on my earlier work on the robustness and security of vision and language models to understand where and why they fail.

Publications

Oral ACL 2026 Student Research Workshop (SRW) · Non-archival

MetaCog-Bench: Quantifying the Metacognition Gap in Edge LLM Tool Calling Under Information Insufficiency

Yu-An Lu, Chun-En Hsiao, Chengwei Chiang, Hong-Han Shuai

A diagnostic benchmark of 4,573 samples across 22 API domains showing that edge LLMs (8B–32B) fabricate plausible tool-call parameters instead of seeking clarification. A structured audit prompt collapses hallucination rates from 32–81% to below 5%, revealing a “metacognition gap” between prompted and default behavior.
